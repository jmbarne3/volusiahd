Only people with an account here can sign in to the admin. Managing accounts needs a superuser; if you aren't one, you won't see Access in the sidebar.

Adding an editor

  1. Open Access → People and press Add person.
  2. Enter the exact email address they use to sign in to Google, and their name. Leave Superuser unticked unless they need to manage accounts too.
  3. Save, then tell them to go to the admin and press Sign in with Google.

Creating the account is what grants access. A Google address without an account here is refused.

Removing an editor

Open their record, untick Active, and Save. It takes effect immediately, even if they're signed in right now.

Deleting the "linked Google account" row under their record doesn't remove access — it only makes their next sign-in match by email again.
