Pages

Pages are the handful of plain pages on the site, like About and FAQ.

  • Published — whether the page can be seen at all.
  • Show in nav — whether it gets a link in the site's header.
  • Sort order — lower numbers come first in the header.

As with programs, avoid changing a page's slug once it's live — it's the page's web address.

Search synonyms

Search finds programs by the exact words in their listing and tags. A family searching "theatre" won't find a program that says "theater" unless you tell search the two words mean the same thing. That's what Search synonyms is for.

  1. Press Add search synonym.
  2. Type the words, separated by commas — for example theater, theatre, drama.
  3. Save. Searching any of them now finds all of them.
  • Single words only — "lego, robotics" works; "lego robotics" as one entry doesn't.
  • Each word can only be in one group. To add a word to a group, edit that group rather than starting a new one.
  • Capitals don't matter.

Words for ages and grades ("tweens", "high schoolers") are handled separately and don't need adding here.
