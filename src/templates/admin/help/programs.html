Programs holds every listing, published or not. This is where you correct a listing after it's live.

Reading the list

  • Status — Published is on the site. Draft is hidden while it's being checked. Archived is hidden because the program no longer runs. You can change it right in the list and press Save at the bottom.
  • Featured — featured programs appear at the top of the home page. Also changeable in the list.
  • On the map — how many of a program's places have a map pin, like "1 of 2". Orange means some don't, so families searching near that place won't find it.
  • Last verified — the last time someone confirmed the listing is right. Orange means over a year ago, or never.

Editing a listing

Open the program, change what you need, and press Save. The description has a small toolbar for bold, lists and links.

Avoid changing the slug. It's the program's web address, so changing it breaks every link and bookmark people already have.

Who to call about this program at the bottom is for our records only. It's never shown on the site.

Putting places on the map

Under Where this program meets, each place is a row. Start typing in the address box and pick a match from the list — that fills in the map pin. If the place isn't in the list, type it anyway and save; the site tries to look it up when you save.

  • To look up several programs' places at once, tick them in the list, choose Look up addresses, and press Run.
  • If a place still can't be found, reword it — a street address usually works better than a building's name — or fill in the latitude and longitude yourself from a map.
  • A city on its own ("DeLand") is a perfectly good answer for a program with no fixed address.

Verifying

When you've confirmed a listing is still right — a phone call, a look at their website — tick it in the list, choose Mark as verified today, and press Run. The date shows on the program's page, so families know the details are current. Applying a suggested edit also counts as verifying.

Taking a program down

  • For a while — set its status to Draft.
  • For good — set it to Archived. It stays in the admin as a record, but not on the site.

Prefer archiving to deleting. A deleted program, and its history, can't be brought back.

To publish several drafts at once, tick them, choose Publish, and press Run.
