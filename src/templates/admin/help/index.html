How to look after the directory: what each screen is for, and how to do the jobs that come up every week.

A normal week

  1. Check Submissions. The number beside it in the sidebar is how many are waiting. Each one also arrives as an email with a link straight to it. See New submissions.
  2. Check Tag suggestions if it has a number beside it. These never hold up a program. See Tag suggestions.
  3. Now and then, look at the "Last verified" column on Programs. Dates in orange are over a year old, or have never been checked. A directory goes stale one listing at a time. See Programs.
